# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-24)

- `screen`: 21 finding codes from FinCEN's November 23, 2026 FTA on Iranian shadow banking, each
  with its page; families and a disposition in which geography alone never leads to review.
- Building keys that drop unit, suite, office and floor numbers; free-zone markers; purpose and
  line-of-business categories.
- `network`: shared-counterparty overlap tested with the hypergeometric tail, hub skipping,
  clusters linked by transfers, buildings and counterparties.
- `develop`: cumulative triangles from case data, chain ladder, Mack (1993) standard errors;
  reproduces the published Taylor and Ashe results.
- `calibrate`: per-code precision with Wilson intervals, recall, lift and a `min_families` sweep.
- Synthetic example generator and data.
- Wording aligned to the FTA after a source check of the November 23, 2026 PDF: the 4,983 figure
  is transactions; corridor order is mainland China, Singapore, unidentified, then the UK; DMCC
  companies "accounted for" about 30 percent of UAE-related funds; company traits apply to many
  and are joined by "or"; source-of-funds is missing or incomplete information, or a mismatch
  with the line of business; FinCEN cited Friedland's chain-ladder chapter, and Mack (1993) is
  this tool's choice for error ranges; Banque Misr UAE is under the August 28, 2026 proposed
  rule only, not a final action.
